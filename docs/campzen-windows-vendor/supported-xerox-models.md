# 5. Supported Commercial Xerox & Multifunction Models

**Device Class**: Enterprise Multifunction Photocopiers & Production Printers  
**Network Protocol**: Standard TCP/IP (Port 9100 RAW / LPR) via Windows Spooler  
**Status**: CERTIFIED FOR CAMPZEN VENDOR  

---

## 1. Certified Xerox Product Families

### Xerox AltaLink Series (High-Volume Campus Production)
- **Models**: AltaLink B8045, B8055, B8070, B8145, B8170 (Monochrome)
- **Models**: AltaLink C8030, C8035, C8045, C8055, C8070, C8130, C8145, C8170 (Colour)
- **Supported Drivers**: Xerox Global Print Driver (PCL 6 & PostScript 3)
- **Features Tested**: Automatic Duplexing, A4/A3 paper switching, stapler finisher unit.

### Xerox WorkCentre Series (Standard Campus Copy Shop)
- **Models**: WorkCentre 5024, 5325, 5330, 5335, 7535, 7845, 7855
- **Features Tested**: Simplex/Duplex, 1200x1200dpi resolution, network RAW spooling.

### Xerox VersaLink Series (Compact Office / Department Counters)
- **Models**: VersaLink B400, B405, B7025, B7030, C400, C7020
- **Features Tested**: High-speed single-pass duplex feeder, Color page ranges.

### Additional PCL 6 Compatible Production Machines
- **HP LaserJet MFP M72625-M72630 PCL 6**: Fully verified on local test network (`172.16.7.1`).
- **Canon imageRUNNER ADVANCE / Konica Minolta bizhub**: Supported via standard Windows PCL 6 / PS drivers.
